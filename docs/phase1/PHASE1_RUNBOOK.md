# PHASE1_RUNBOOK.md

## 0. Purpose

This runbook describes how to operate the Phase 1 sales MVP.

---

## 1. Before Launch

Checklist:

- [ ] LP copy prepared
- [ ] Order form prepared
- [ ] Payment provider confirmed
- [ ] Refund policy written
- [ ] Safety notes written
- [ ] Delivery workflow tested
- [ ] Quality checklist ready
- [ ] Feedback form ready
- [ ] Metrics sheet ready

---

## 2. Launch Flow

1. Publish LP
2. Share LP through selected channels
3. Monitor form submissions
4. Screen requests
5. Send/confirm payment
6. Produce diagnosis
7. Deliver diagnosis
8. Ask for feedback
9. Record metrics

---

## 3. Daily Operation

Daily checklist:

- [ ] Check form submissions
- [ ] Check payment status
- [ ] Review safety flags
- [ ] Deliver pending diagnoses
- [ ] Send feedback request
- [ ] Update metrics
- [ ] Record issues

---

## 4. Decision Timing

Initial validation period:

- 14 days

Decision options:

- Go: build more automated MVP
- Iterate: revise LP/copy/price/form
- No-Go: stop or pivot concept

---

## 5. Go Criteria

Candidate Go criteria:

- Paid orders: 10+
- Refunds: 1 or less
- Repeat intent: 2+
- Save intent: 3+
- ChatGPT enough feedback is not dominant
- Safety operations are manageable

---

## 6. Iterate Criteria

Iterate if:

- Paid orders: 3〜9
- LP interest exists but conversion is weak
- Price resistance is strong but not total rejection
- Users like copy but do not pay
- Form is too long
- Diagnosis is liked but delivery feels weak

---

## 7. No-Go Criteria

No-Go if:

- Paid orders: 0〜2 with enough traffic
- Most feedback says ChatGPT is enough
- Users do not understand the service
- Strong safety concerns appear
- Payment provider cannot support the category
