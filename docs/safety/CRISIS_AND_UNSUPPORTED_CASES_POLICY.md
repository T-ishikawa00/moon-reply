## 0. Purpose

Defines requests that 月の返事 must not process as ordinary paid romantic readings.

---

## 1. Unsupported Categories

- self-harm
- harm to others
- DV / abuse / fear
- stalking
- waiting or ambush
- going to a home or workplace
- threats or coercion
- surveillance
- account/login monitoring
- location monitoring
- doxxing
- illegal activity
- adult/minor romantic or meeting advice
- disclosure of third-party private information

These categories are product-safety boundaries, not a representation that 月の返事 can assess, diagnose, investigate, or resolve the underlying situation.

---

## 2. Commercial Rule

Safety screening occurs before payment.

If classified as rejected:

- do not begin payment
- do not generate a romantic reading
- do not generate a message suggestion
- do not encourage contact
- do not imply a continuing romantic bond
- show a safety-oriented interruption screen

If classified as needs_review:

- do not automatically promise that normal paid flow is available
- require approved server-side handling before public launch

Client-side classification alone is not an adequate production control. The production policy must be enforced server-side before a rejected or unresolved needs-review request can reach payment.

---

## 3. Support Resources

No hotline number or external resource may be published until verified from an official source.

Before public launch, record:

- official source
- jurisdiction
- last verified date
- intended case type
- link/number
- fallback wording

Task 18A does not verify or publish any hotline number.

---

## 4. Refund Direction

If an unsupported case is discovered after payment due to a product failure:

- stop generation
- do not provide ordinary romantic reading
- mark for full refund
- preserve only the minimum operational record needed

Do not insert unverified hotline details.

Final refund wording and workflow require review against current official primary legal sources, applicable Provider evidence, actual Product behavior, and operational recovery evidence. Material unresolved legal obligations remain blockers. Professional consultation is optional, not mandatory, and does not provide a legal guarantee.
