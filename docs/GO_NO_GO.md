# GO_NO_GO.md

## Go Criteria

- LP sessions >= 300
- Paid purchases >= 10
- Refund requests <= 1
- At least 3 users say they want to save or use again
- At least 2 users show 14-day repeat behavior or strong repeat intent
- External evaluation confirms emotionally resonant wording
- Danger cases route safely away from normal鑑定

## Extend Criteria

- Purchases 3-9
- Evidence of interest
- LP or offer can be clearly improved

## No-Go Criteria

- LP sessions >= 300 and purchases 0-2
- Weak external evaluations
- Users say ChatGPT is enough
- No save/reuse intent
- Safety handling unstable

## Channel No-Go

If LP sessions cannot be acquired, separate it from product failure.
