## 5. Refund Policy Requirements

Required sections:

- Before Payment: no charge
- After successful Payment and objectively before `generation_started`: full JPY 980 ordinary refund only when server-authoritative evidence establishes timing
- Successful generation
- Product/technical failure: same-Purchase recovery, no recharge, then full JPY 980 ordinary refund if fulfillment remains impossible
- Payment Integrity: duplicate charge, wrong amount/Customer/Request, Safety bypass, and another unintended economic charge; actual error corrected without a JPY 980 cap
- Wrong email: ownership verification, correction, old-credential invalidation where necessary, secure reissue, Privacy incident evaluation, and full-refund candidacy when secure fulfillment remains impossible
- User dissatisfaction alone
- Chargeback handling
- Refund processing time: `TBD`

Refund wording and applicable statutory limits must be marked for current-source, Customer-rights, actual-Product, and Provider-evidence review. Internal review does not guarantee legal compliance.
