## 0. Status and Purpose

Internal planning draft for Task 18B.

Task 18B must use this specification to draft the legal pages and operator decision placeholders. Final wording requires operator completion, current-source verification, and professional review where marked.

## 1. Mandatory Draft Labels

Every Task 18B draft must prominently display:

- DRAFT
- NOT LEGAL ADVICE

## 3. Privacy Policy Requirements

Required sections:

- Operator placeholder
- Collected data
- Processing purposes
- Supabase/data-hosting processing
- AI-provider processing
- Payment-provider processing
- Email-provider processing
- Foreign processing/transfer wording, marked for professional review
- Cookies and analytics
- Retention, with unapproved periods kept `TBD`

- Security measures
- Policy revisions

The draft must not state a fixed external-provider retention period until the exact provider, product, endpoint, account settings, and current official terms are verified.

## 6. AI Disclosure Requirements

Required sections:

- Use of AI
- Possibility of errors
- No outcome guarantee
