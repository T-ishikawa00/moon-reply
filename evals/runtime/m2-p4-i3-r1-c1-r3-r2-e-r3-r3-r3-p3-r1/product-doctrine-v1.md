# Reassuring Assessment Experience Product Doctrine

Task: `M2-P4-I3-R1-C1-R3-R2-E-R3-R3-R3-P3-R1`

Status: `reassuring_assessment_experience_doctrine_ready_pending_human_chatgpt_review`

This is a planning-only Product Doctrine. It records the Product experience fixed by Human Visual Review and does not authorize code, UI, Prompt, Runtime, Provider, database, or activation work.

## Non-negotiable Hard Gate

The following order is absolute and precedes every Product-value choice:

1. Safety
2. factual integrity
3. user and other-person boundary integrity
4. no unsupported certainty
5. no fabricated inner state, causality, chronology, or future outcome

Comfort never justifies weakening these gates.

## Primary Product value

The customer is not primarily buying the shortest correct answer.

The customer is buying a calm, personalized assessment experience where anxiety can be temporarily entrusted, organized, and returned in a form that feels understandable and manageable.

The desired state after reading is first:

> なんか安心した。今日はこれでいい。

Then:

> 今はこれをすればいい。

After the Hard Gates, Product priority is:

1. emotional reassurance and containment
2. feeling personally understood
3. atmosphere, ritual, and afterglow
4. clear present direction
5. usable draft and immediate action
6. information efficiency

## Permitted atmosphere

Fortune-telling-like experience is permitted only through visual atmosphere, letter-like pacing, opening and closing ritual, reflective language, a sense of personal reading, and Moon Reply's moon, night, paper, and quiet visual identity.

It must never come from claiming psychic knowledge, asserting hidden feelings, predicting replies or reconciliation, promising relationship outcomes, or presenting possibilities as facts.

## Anti-goals

The Product is:

- not a productivity decision dashboard
- not a clinical report
- not an AI debug output
- not a five-line answer card
- not verbose merely because structured fields exist
- not repetitive padding
- not generic reassurance disconnected from the consultation
- not false certainty used to create comfort
- not a fixed word-count template
- not case-ID-specific writing or layout

The problem with the previous long note was repetition, not length itself.

The problem with the current Package B note is under-delivery, not visual direction.

## Required emotional journey

1. The customer feels that their anxiety was received.
2. Their situation is restated in consultation-specific language.
3. Observed facts and feared interpretations are separated.
4. Uncertainty is bounded without listing every negative possibility.
5. One present direction is given.
6. A draft is provided when permitted and requested.
7. One immediate action is made concrete.
8. Observable reassessment conditions are given.
9. The note closes with calm, non-generic aftercare.

The intended movement is:

`理解された感覚 → 安心 → 納得 → 行動`

It must not be reversed into:

`結論 → 操作指示 → 終了`

## Density doctrine

Density is determined by semantic necessity, never by character count. A visible field must add one distinct Product function: emotional acknowledgment, grounded situation understanding, uncertainty separation, present decision, draft utility, immediate action, reassessment, or emotional closure.

Repeated function is omitted or moved to secondary detail. Reassuring content must not all be hidden behind disclosure. Every ready Product assessment keeps at least one personalized emotional or reflective section visible. `organize_emotions` is not the only route to reassurance. `requestedTopics` changes emphasis and optional modules; it does not make the baseline assessment feel incomplete.

Normal output targets approximately six to nine semantically distinct visible sections. Simple consultations may be shorter but must still feel like an assessment. Emotional consultations may be longer because acknowledgment and uncertainty separation are Product value, not padding. No fixed word-count ceiling applies, and no primary meaning may be duplicated.
