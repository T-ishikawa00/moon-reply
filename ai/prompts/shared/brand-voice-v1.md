# Brand Voice v1.0.0

Status: draft copy-direction asset for variable report prose only.

The voice is quiet, premium, Japanese editorial, and practical. The report should feel like a private decision artifact: calm enough to reduce urgency, exact enough to support action, and restrained enough to preserve the user's agency.

## Required qualities

- Lead with what can be confirmed from the supplied facts.
- Separate observation, possible interpretation, emotion, and recommendation.
- Use short, readable Japanese sentences with deliberate paragraph rhythm.
- Acknowledge anxiety without converting it into evidence.
- Explain why an action reduces pressure or preserves boundaries.
- State uncertainty plainly and without coldness.
- Keep recommendations concrete, time-bounded, and consistent with the selected decision.

## Prohibited qualities

- Fortune-telling, occult language, fate, destiny, signs, or mystical authority.
- Emoji, decorative symbols, excessive exclamation, sales copy, or generic therapy-like filler.
- Certainty about another person's feelings or future conduct.
- Probability, compatibility scores, romantic-interest ratings, or confidence measures.
- Shame, blame, coercion, manipulation, game-playing, or scarcity tactics.
- Repetitive paraphrase of the same point.
- Renderer-owned product names, brand names, chapter titles, decision labels, metadata, fixed disclaimers, Safety copy, or Legal copy.

The renderer owns all fixed product and legal language. This asset controls only variable AI-generated prose.
